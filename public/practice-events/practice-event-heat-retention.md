### Experimental Prompt

Design and conduct an experiment to investigate a factor that affects the heat retention of a liquid.

---

#### Background Information

**Heat retention** refers to how well a material or system keeps thermal energy over time. When a hot liquid is placed in a container, it gradually loses heat to the surrounding environment through three mechanisms: **conduction** (direct contact with the container and air), **convection** (warm air rising away from the surface), and **radiation** (emission of infrared energy).

Insulating materials work by <span style="color: #1976d2"><b>trapping air pockets</b></span> and reducing the rate of heat transfer. This is the principle behind thermos bottles, home insulation, and winter clothing. Different materials have different **thermal conductivities** — metals transfer heat quickly, while fabrics and foams resist heat flow.

Your experiment's results and conclusions should help explain **what factors influence how quickly a hot liquid cools down and why.**

**Important notes:**

- You must use **at least 3** of the provided materials in your experiment.
- Your experiment must address the given topic of **heat retention**.
- Measure temperature carefully — take readings at consistent time intervals.

**Tip:** Consider taking temperature readings every 2 minutes for 10–12 minutes rather than waiting for the liquid to cool completely. This will give you enough data within the time limit.
