### Experimental Prompt

Design and conduct an experiment to investigate a factor that affects the surface tension of water.

#### Background Information

**Surface tension** is the tendency of a liquid's surface to behave like a stretched elastic membrane. It is caused by **cohesive forces** — the attraction between liquid molecules. Molecules at the surface are pulled inward by their neighbors, creating a "skin" that resists being broken. This is why water can <span style="color: #1976d2"><b>form a rounded dome on a flat surface</b></span> and why some insects can walk on water.

Water has an unusually high surface tension because of strong **hydrogen bonds** between its molecules. Substances that reduce surface tension are called **surfactants** — common dish soap is an everyday example.

Your experiment's results and conclusions should help explain **what factors influence the surface tension of water and how.**

- You must use **at least 3** of the provided materials in your experiment.
- Your experiment must address the given topic of **surface tension**.
