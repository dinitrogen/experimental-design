## The Experiment — Toy Car on a Ramp

**Problem:** How does the height of a ramp (cm) affect the distance (cm) a toy car rolls along the floor after leaving the ramp?

- **IV:** Ramp height (cm), tested at 10, 20, 30, and 40 cm
- **DV:** Distance the car rolled past the bottom of the ramp (cm), measured with a meterstick
- **Trials:** 5 trials at each ramp height

### Raw measurements from the team's lab notebook

| Ramp Height | Distances Recorded (cm) |
|:-----------:|:------------------------|
| 10 cm | 45, 42, 48, 45, 50 |
| 20 cm | 92, 88, 95, 92, 93 |
| 30 cm | 135, 130, 138, 142, 135 |
| 40 cm | 165, 160, 172, 170, 163 |

*Values are listed in trial order (Trial 1 first).*

## Part H — Graph

Plan the graph for this experiment. Then **draw it on graph paper** with a ruler, and show it to your coach or teacher. Remember:

- The **IV** goes on the **x-axis** and the **DV** goes on the **y-axis**.
- Plot **one point for each IV level**, then draw **one line of best fit** (not connect-the-dots).
- Use an **even scale** with **units** on both axes.

## Part I — Statistics

Your team will calculate statistics for **every IV level**. First, work all the way through the **30 cm** level step by step. This is the **sample calculation** a judge wants to see.

- Report answers to **one decimal place** where needed.
- Use the **sample** standard deviation (divide by $n - 1$).
- To find Q1 and Q3 with 5 values, leave out the median, then find the median of the lower half and of the upper half.
