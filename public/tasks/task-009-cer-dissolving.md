# Full CER Practice: Effect of Water Temperature on Sugar Dissolving Time

## Experiment Summary

A team investigated how **water temperature** affects the **time it takes for sugar to dissolve**. A 5-gram measure of granulated sugar was added to 200 mL of water at three different temperatures and stirred at a constant rate. Each temperature was tested five times under identical conditions.

**IV:** Water Temperature (°C) &nbsp;&nbsp; **DV:** Dissolving Time (s)
**Controlled Variables:** Same amount of sugar (5 g), same volume of water (200 mL), same stirring rate, same type of sugar, same beaker

**Hypothesis:** If water temperature increases, then the time for sugar to dissolve will decrease because higher temperatures increase the kinetic energy of water molecules, allowing them to break apart the sugar crystal lattice more quickly.
