# Report Writing Practice: Sections A–E

## Experiment Prompt — Buoyancy

**Topic:** Buoyancy & Density

**Background:** Buoyancy is the upward force a fluid exerts on an object placed in it. An object floats when the buoyant force balances its weight, and the more weight it carries, the lower it sits in the water. You are prospective ship designers who need to know how much cargo a boat can carry safely. Design an experiment that explores buoyancy using the materials below. You do not have to use every item.

**Materials:**

- Large clear plastic tub
- Water
- 3 plastic cups (small, medium, large)
- 40 pennies
- Waterproof marker
- Masking tape
- Ruler
- Mass balance
- Calculator
- Paper towels
