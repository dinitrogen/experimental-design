# Quick Design Challenge

For each topic below, come up with an experiment you could conduct using at least 3 of the the provided materials (plus ruler/tape measure, stopwatch, kitchen scale and calculators).

For each topic, write:
- A **statement of problem**
- Your **independent variable**
- Your **dependent variable**

Don't forget units on your IV and DV!

These should be done quickly like in the real event, don't spend more than a few minutes on each one!

---

**Materials:** 3 rubber bands (different widths), binder clip, paper cup, pennies, tape
