# Report Writing Practice: Sections A–E

## Experiment Prompt — Friction

**Topic:** Friction & Motion

**Background:** Friction is a force that resists the motion of two surfaces sliding past each other. It depends on the surfaces that are touching and on how hard they are pressed together. You are prospective engineers choosing the best material for a playground slide. Design an experiment that explores friction using the materials below. You do not have to use every item.

**Materials:**

- Smooth wooden board (60 cm long)
- Wooden block
- 4 textbooks
- Sandpaper sheet
- Wax paper sheet
- Aluminum foil sheet
- Felt fabric piece
- 10 pennies
- Masking tape
- Ruler
- Meterstick
- Protractor
- Stopwatch
- Calculator
- Spring scale
- String
- Safety goggles
