# Quick Design Challenge: States Prep

For each topic below, come up with an experiment you could conduct using at least 3 of the provided materials (plus ruler/tape measure, stopwatch, kitchen scale, and calculators).

For each topic, write:
- A **statement of problem**
- A **hypothesis** (If... then... because...)
- Your **independent variable** (with at least 3 levels and units)
- Your **dependent variable** (with units)
- At least **3 controlled variables** (with how each is controlled)

These should be done quickly like in the real event — don't spend more than a few minutes on each one!
