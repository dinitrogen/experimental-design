# Full CER Practice: Effect of Wing Length on Paper Helicopter Fall Time

## Experiment Summary

A team investigated how **wing length** affects the **fall time** of a paper helicopter. They constructed paper helicopters with three different wing lengths and dropped each from a height of 2 meters. Each wing length was tested five times under identical conditions.

**IV:** Wing Length (cm) &nbsp;&nbsp; **DV:** Fall Time (s)
**Controlled Variables:** Same paper type, same drop height (2 m), same drop technique, same indoor location (no wind)

**Hypothesis:** If wing length increases, then fall time will increase because longer wings create more air resistance, slowing the helicopter's descent.
