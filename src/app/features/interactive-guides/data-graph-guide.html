4. Graph and line of best fit

Choose scales and label axes with units. Plot each IV level and its entered mean; use two points on your drawn line, not necessarily data points, to find its equation. Free placement and exact coordinates are available below.

How to calculate the line of best fit

Choose two points on your line. Calculate the slope, m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁), then the intercept, b = y₁ − mx₁. The equation is y = mx + b.
