4. Graph and line of best fit

Choose scales and label axes with units. Plot each IV level and its entered mean; use two points on your drawn line, not necessarily data points, to find its equation. Free placement and exact coordinates are available below.

What does y = mx + b mean?

x is the independent variable and y is the predicted dependent variable. The slope m is the change in y for each one-unit increase in x, in y-units per x-unit. The intercept b is the predicted y when x = 0, in y-units.

A positive slope rises; a negative slope falls. The intercept may not be meaningful if x = 0 is outside the experiment’s tested range. A best-fit line summarizes a trend; it does not prove causation or guarantee predictions outside that range.
